Course fees at a glance

These are the fee rules confirmed for SIFA. Individual invoices and any approved scholarship are confirmed by authorized SIFA staff.

Standard monthly fee

Monthly course fee

10,000 RWF

Standard monthly fee per enrolled learner. Monthly billing is handled by SIFA staff, not through automatic recurring charges.

Optional

Course-completion certificate

5,000 RWF

Only charged if a learner requests a certificate after meeting SIFA's completion requirements. This fee is separate from monthly tuition and is not covered by monthly-fee scholarships.

Payment due date: the first day of each month. Grace period: 14 days after the due date, subject to the final verified invoice and access rules. Renewals: handled manually by SIFA staff; no automatic renewal charge is taken.

Scholarships and fee support

SIFA may announce partial reductions to monthly course fees when a specific opportunity is officially opened. Scholarship information is public, and you do not need to log in to read the conditions.

What support covers

Approved support reduces monthly course fees only, according to the terms confirmed in the official opportunity announcement.

Eligibility and decisions

Requests are assessed against the criteria, deadlines and availability announced for each opportunity. Submitting a request does not guarantee an award.

What support does not cover

Scholarships do not cover the optional 5,000 RWF certificate fee. That fee is payable separately only if a learner requests a certificate.

Current scholarship status: no scholarship opportunity is currently open for applications. SIFA will publish eligibility requirements, reduction amounts, deadlines and application instructions when an opportunity is officially opened. We do not promise a full scholarship or invent an award percentage that has not been announced.

Frequently asked questions

Can I read the payment and scholarship information without logging in?

Yes. This entire information page is public. Signing in is required only for private student records, invoices, and submitting a receipt tied to your account.

Does submitting a receipt automatically activate my course?

No. An authorized reviewer must verify and approve the payment or an applicable scholarship/fee waiver before course access can be granted.

When is the monthly fee due, and is renewal automatic?

The monthly fee is due on the first day of each month. SIFA staff handle monthly renewals manually; there is no automatic charge.

How does the grace period work?

A 14-day grace period applies after the due date. The student's actual access status depends on verified invoice status and the configured access policy. Ask SIFA if you need confirmation for your account.

Can a scholarship pay for my certificate?

No. Scholarships reduce monthly course fees only. The optional certificate fee is 5,000 RWF and is payable separately if you request a certificate after meeting the completion requirements.

Are scholarship applications open now?

No. There is currently no open scholarship opportunity. SIFA will publish specific criteria, reduction amounts and deadlines when an opportunity opens.

What if I paid in a different currency?

Confirm the correct invoice currency and amount with SIFA before making the transfer. Do not calculate or assume a conversion rate yourself.

Where can I verify the official payment details?

Email sifaglobalinstitute12@gmail.com or use the Contact page. Verify the recipient and amount before transferring money.
